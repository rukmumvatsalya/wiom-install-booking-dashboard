      Day by screen

      How these numbers are counted
      • Start: the customer’s first App Installed event on or after the “Installed from” date. Each customer belongs to the day they installed (IST).
      • A screen counts if the customer reached it within the chosen number of days after installing, at any point, even after going back and forth.
      • “Booking fee paid” and “Booking confirmed” are the backend records (booking_fee_captured, booking_confirmed); the rest are app screen events.
      • New customers leaves out anyone who, in the same window, tapped “login karein”, saw the existing-connection screen, or paid a renewal. About one install in five is an existing customer.
      • Today’s flow (J5) asks for the date before the address. When J6 goes live its customers see the address first; the order here stays J5’s.
      • The warehouse runs about a day behind the app, and install days younger than the window are still counting.
      • “Earlier installs” on the fee-paid and booked cards: customers who paid the booking fee between “Installed from” and yesterday but installed the app before that date (or no install was seen). They are counted by payment day from the backend booking records, kept apart from the install funnel, and include existing customers.

      How these numbers are counted
      • Every number is distinct customers in the app's CleverTap events, from the date picked at the top. The warehouse runs about a day behind the app.
      • D7 and D30: D7 counts customers in the first 7 days from the release date (release day = day 1); D30 the first 30 days. The 90% target is judged on D30. Until a window has run its full length it shows the days so far. Release dates are set at the top; J6's is its first J6 fee payment.
      • "Before" for Wiom TV is the old promo sheet in the 7 / 30 days just before release. For J6 it is J5 fee payers in the same 7 / 30 days, since both run side by side.
      • "Of start" is customers on a screen ÷ customers on the first screen. "From previous" is customers on a screen ÷ customers on the screen before it. Below 90% is red.
      • Wiom TV gift: success = tapped Watch ÷ opened the gift page. Before = tapped "watch free" ÷ saw the old promo sheet, on the same dates.
      • R-day survey: success = answer saved ÷ opened question 1. Question 2 counts customers who saw either the difficulty or the reason question.
      • J6 vs J5: customers whose fee payment was tagged J5 or J6, and which later screens they reached after paying.
      • "Sent" is from the chat message log (message orchestrator): customers the workflow wrote the message for. The log does not record delivery, so "reached" is read from the app: the customer opened chat after the message was sent.
      • "Saw message" is the app's message_from_wiom_delivered event for that workflow, plus anyone who tapped its button. "Tapped" is wiom_message_cta_clicked on the message's button.
      • Message log and app events are matched on the customer's mobile number, and only app events after the send time count.
      • The A/B split for the Wiom TV gift is decided on the server, so these events do not show which customers were held back.

      How these are counted
      • Address drop-off: of customers who paid the booking fee (backend booking_fee_captured, as Metabase dashboard 1280), the share with no address saved after that payment. The current month leaves out today and yesterday, whose payers are still submitting.
      • When customers call: the stage Wiom’s support line records on each call (LAST_STAGE): before the slot date, on it, within the installation grace period, after it, after the booking was cancelled, or anything else (tickets, net, plan).
      • Calls to Wiom: customer calls to Wiom’s support line between the engineer being assigned and installation. Calls to the engineer: the customer’s masked-number calls to their CSP in the same window; “didn’t pick up” = no answer.
      • The window runs from the engineer being assigned to installation (COMPANY_B_CONNECTION_BOOKING_ENRICHED assigned and install times; still open if not yet installed). Calls to Wiom are distinct calls to Wiom’s support line from the customer’s number in that window: October = 5,239, the data science team’s figure exactly. Calls to the engineer read 1,817 for October here against the team’s 1,232, and 35 not picked up against 38, so those two are counted a little more widely.
